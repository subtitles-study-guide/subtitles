- 狩ら (kara) — to hunt (animals)
- れ (re) — indicates passive voice (incl. the "suffering passive")
- て (te) — hand / arm
- いる (iru) — to be / exist (animate)
- の (no) — possessive / linking particle
- は (ha) — topic marker
- その (sono) — that / the
- 心 (kokoro) — mind / heart / spirit
